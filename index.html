A clear conversation starts with a specific idea.

Specific beats impressive. “A booking page connected to WhatsApp” tells a clearer story than “an innovative digital solution.”

Better pitches begin with better questions.Explore the thinking behind SaleScript

Give the idea a clear shape

Start with a specific audience, a recognizable problem and a useful outcome. Explain the difference in your approach without burying the idea in features.

Give people a reason to listen

Try a relevant question or a fresh perspective. Add a real result and a genuine reason for the timing when you have them.

Make the next step easy

Keep the opening focused, invite a response and ask for one clear next step. A confident pitch leaves room for both people to decide whether it fits.
